# Institutional Ethics Approval & Participant Consent Statement

## 1. Ethical Review and Institutional Clearance

This study was conducted in strict accordance with the ethical principles outlined in the **Declaration of Helsinki** for research involving human participants.

- **Review Board:** Departmental Research Ethics & Academic Project Committee (DREC)
- **Institution:** Department of Computer Science and Engineering, Presidency University, Dhaka, Bangladesh
- **Approval Protocol ID:** `DREC-2025-EDM-014`
- **Approval Date:** January 15, 2025
- **Study Title:** *Explainable Machine Learning for Student Burnout Prediction: An Explanatory Sequential Mixed-Methods Study with Qualitative Triangulation*

---

## 2. Participant Informed Consent

### Quantitative Survey Strand ($N = 601$)
- Prior to completing the survey instrument via Google Forms, all participants were presented with an electronic Informed Consent Form detailing:
  1. The explicit educational and research purpose of the study.
  2. The voluntary nature of participation, with full freedom to withdraw at any point without penalty or academic consequence.
  3. Strict confidentiality and de-identification measures applied to all behavioral and psychometric survey responses.
- Electronic consent was mandatory before accessing survey items.

### Qualitative Interview Strand ($N = 20$)
- Audio recording and verbatim transcription were conducted only after obtaining explicit written/verbal informed consent.
- Participants were assigned standardized pseudonyms (e.g., *Participant 1 [P1]*, *Participant 20 [P20]*, *Private University A*, *National University System*) to protect participant identities.
- Participant quotes included in published outputs were stripped of all personally identifying details.

---

## 3. Data Protection and Anonymization

- All primary survey responses were fully de-identified (`Quantitative_Survey_Data.xlsx`).
- Raw qualitative interview transcripts were anonymized and stored on password-protected, encrypted local storage accessible solely to the primary research team.
- Publicly deposited repository artifacts retain full anonymity in compliance with GDPR and local institutional ethics guidelines.
